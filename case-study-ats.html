Case study · Healthcare hiring platform · In progress

ATS Application.

A hiring platform for healthcare organizations such as hospitals, clinics and staffing agencies. It takes a role from the first request to hire all the way through to onboarding, in one system, with control over who can see what.

The problem

Healthcare employers hire many clinical and non-clinical roles across several sites. Every hire needs approvals and compliance checks, and it has to happen quickly. When that work is spread across spreadsheets, emails and disconnected tools, nobody has one clear view of where a role or a candidate stands, and it is hard to control who can see what.

This project replaces that with a single system. Hiring managers, recruiters, HR and candidates all work in the same place, and each sees only what their role allows.

The hiring flow

The product follows a role from the first request to hire all the way through onboarding.

  1. Raise a requisitionA hiring manager requests a hire, for one or more locations.
  2. ApprovalThe requisition goes through an approval process before any recruiting starts.
  3. Assign a team and set up the jobA recruiting team is assigned, and the job is set up through intake.
  4. Post the jobThe job is published to the careers site and to other job boards.
  5. Receive and shortlist candidatesCandidates apply, and recruiters search, rank and shortlist them.
  6. Assess and verifyCandidates go through assessments and interviews, plus credential and license verification.
  7. Offer and onboardOffers are made, and the candidate moves into preboarding and onboarding.

Roles and responsibilities

Access in the system is organised around four internal roles. Each one has its own responsibilities and its own level of access.

Role 1Super Admin

Full system control and configuration. Manages all users, roles, modules and system settings.

  • Manage system-wide settings and configurations
  • Create, update and deactivate organizations
  • Manage all users and roles
  • Define role-based access permissions
  • Manage modules, features and system templates
  • Manage job boards, career sites and integrations
  • Monitor system activity, audit logs and performance
  • Manage data backup and system security
Role 2Chief Human Resources Officer

Sets HR strategy and oversees the talent acquisition and people management process.

  • Define HR strategy and recruitment goals
  • Approve workforce planning and hiring budgets
  • Monitor talent acquisition metrics and KPIs
  • Review and approve job requisitions and key hiring decisions
  • Oversee diversity, equity and inclusion initiatives
  • Ensure compliance with labor laws and HR policies
  • Review reports, analytics and dashboards
Role 3Head HR

Leads HR operations and ensures effective implementation of HR policies, processes and people management.

  • Oversee the end-to-end recruitment and selection process
  • Approve job requisitions and job descriptions
  • Monitor the hiring pipeline and time-to-fill metrics
  • Coordinate between HR, hiring managers and recruiters
  • Manage employee data and HR records
  • Analyze HR reports and recommend improvements
  • Manage training and development programs
Role 4HR

Executes HR processes, manages candidates and ensures a smooth hiring and employee onboarding experience.

  • Create and post job openings
  • Screen resumes and shortlist candidates
  • Schedule interviews and coordinate with stakeholders
  • Communicate with candidates throughout the process
  • Maintain candidate and employee records
  • Onboard new hires and manage documentation
  • Update the recruitment pipeline and maintain reports

How responsibility flows

Decisions move down the chain and results move back up. Each role builds on the one above it.

  1. Super AdminConfigures the system, manages users, roles and permissions.
  2. →
  3. Chief Human Resources OfficerSets strategy, approves plans and monitors performance.
  4. →
  5. Head HRImplements strategies, oversees HR operations and ensures process excellence.
  6. →
  7. HRExecutes day-to-day HR activities and keeps the experience smooth for candidates and employees.

How it is built

The front end is built with Next.js, and the back end with Python. Data is stored in PostgreSQL, accessed through SQLAlchemy with the psycopg driver, and Redis sits alongside the database. AI parsing is part of the system as well.
